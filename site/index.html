Read the story in English

Imagine I came up with a lamp that turns away when I open my work chat at night. You’re like, “I’ll just take a quick peek.” But it’s already facing the wall. Fun, right?

I throw the idea at an AI. It immediately goes: “Great! Audience, price, subscription. We launch on Friday.” Hold on. All I’ve invented so far is a sulking lamp. Where did Friday come from? I haven’t even decided whether it needs an “okay, five more minutes” button. And we already have a lighting subscription.

I say, “Let’s just imagine how it turns for now.” The reply: “Of course. Let’s start with a technical specification.” One question would have been enough: are we dreaming, or are we getting started?

Okay, I’m showing the lamp our plan. There. It turned away again.
