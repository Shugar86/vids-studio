# Create and review a Google Vids video

Deliver a coherent video that serves the viewer's purpose, with an editable Vids
project. Technical validity and accurate transcription support that result; they
do not establish a clear idea, expressive action, or good pacing. Assemble scenes,
timing, sound and the final edit in Vids. Local images, graphics and authorized
short motion inserts may be prepared elsewhere and imported. This does not imply
permission to add a provider, install a renderer, or move the whole film to a
different editor. An already authorized mixed workflow needs no repeated approval.

## Choose the next useful step

* **New video:** infer the audience, viewing context, central idea, language,
  format and available material. For promotion, identify the offer, evidence and
  viewer action; see [craft](craft.md). Use settled choices. Ask one focused
  question only for a material missing fact, while continuing independent work.
* **Editorial channel or series:** read [editorial-series](editorial-series.md).
  Preserve the author's reasoning and voice; do not turn every episode into an ad.
  When episodes are produced from a topic plan without hand-directing each one,
  add [series production](series-production.md): a series kit, an episode card
  checked by `series_card.py`, and a gated pipeline with a budget.
* **Targeted edit:** inspect the existing video and adjacent scenes. Preserve
  working choices and fix the named issue; a caption edit does not imply a redesign.
* **“Make it better” or a substantial creative revision:** establish a direction
  and representative sample before expensive batch generation. For a dynamic
  short video, sample the opening through its first completed event, not only a
  later climax. Distinguish a weak idea, absent action, repetitive composition and
  poor execution. If creative choice is delegated, explain the decision and act.
* **The same complaint returns after an edit:** compare the complaint, your
  earlier explanation and what actually changed. New captions do not test a
  hypothesis about missing character movement. Revise the explanation and test
  another supported cause; stop dependent work based on a disproved assumption.
  Preserve accepted parts and the unresolved issue. Separate local defects do
  not require restarting the whole direction; distinguish a new request from an
  unmet earlier requirement.
* **Images, clips or avatars:** use [generation](generation.md). A portrait,
  talking script-avatar and personal avatar are different deliverables.
* **A character and a moving object:** read [action and reaction](action-and-reaction.md)
  before generation: joint framing, hands, object mechanics, reaction order,
  pacing and take selection.
* **Sound, captions and cuts after editing:** use [timing and caption QA](timing-and-caption-qa.md)
  for SFX alignment, spoken/visible repetition, local caption trims, a second
  subtitle track and frame-level rechecks. A single take's observations are not
  a universal cut offset or fixed QA window.

* **Diagnosis only:** remain read-only unless a fix is requested.

## Build the film

1. State the promise to the viewer. Give each scene a purpose, something visible,
   something heard and a meaningful change by its end. Remove redundant beats.
2. Separate spoken copy from concise screen copy. Use real product/data evidence
   when a claim needs proof; label mockups or generated illustrations honestly.
3. Choose format before layout. Establish typography, spacing, color and a
   representative scene, then vary composition when the meaning changes.
4. Edit to speech and action. A complete voice phrase may span multiple shots;
   changing shots does not require regenerating TTS. Silent work is timed by
   reading and visible action. Do not leave every scene at a default duration.
5. Check voice, music, clip sound and captions separately. Read
   [speech and captions](russian-and-speech.md) before speech generation or caption
   work; language availability depends on the specific feature and account.
6. Review the actual result, fix confirmed problems and deliver it. Use
   [review-and-delivery](review-and-delivery.md) for full coverage and independent
   viewer critique. A narrow edit requires its affected range and neighboring
   entrances/exits; a new or substantially re-edited video requires full coverage.

Use the local [take manifest](take-manifest.md) when selecting or replacing takes.
Keep source/hash/range, selection reasons and evidence. Replacement reopens
checks of timing, speech, both caption types, music, SFX and neighboring shots.
A local plan does not prove the editor matches it.

## Operate within the attached document

Read [operate-vids](operate-vids.md) before acting. Use the selected client's Vids
MCP and current schemas. This is guarded UI control, not a scene/timeline API.
Mutations use the current session/control/snapshot and a new operation ID.
Derive targets from fresh observation; never invent selectors or functions.

Work in the background without system mouse, keyboard, clipboard or foreground
activation. One agent controls the attached document; others prepare material
and review evidence without its token. Do not take over busy control. If the MCP
is unavailable, continue independent preparation and report the connection step
needed. Do not silently install extensions, change profiles or switch providers.

Successful text input does not prove a correct title; a visible waveform does
not prove an audible voice; a generation button does not prove a finished asset.
Use the evidence appropriate to the claim. [Sources](sources.md) are entry points
for verification, not promises about the current account or a hidden Vids API.
