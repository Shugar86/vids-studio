# Sources and limits of knowledge

These are official entry points for checking a feature, not promises of account
access or output quality. UI, languages, models, plans and limits change: consult
the specific current page and visible controls before use. Repository tests
exercise runtime/helpers; they do not establish generated video quality, heard
pronunciation or current Google account availability. Private production histories
and source artifacts are not part of this public package.

Do not transfer language support between voiceover, script-avatar, personal
avatar, avatar ingredient and image ingredient. Closed and styled captions need
separate checks. A language listing does not establish accurate words, sync or
visibility in an MP4. When a general article conflicts with a specific feature
page, verify that feature and perform a short observed account test. An absent
catalogue entry is not proof that no API could exist.

## Public ideas and provenance

[HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) informs the practice of
checking a complete still composition before animation. Only an available,
authorized runtime is used; upstream runtime code and fonts are not bundled here.
[OpenTimelineIO](https://opentimelineio.readthedocs.io/en/latest/tutorials/otio-timeline-structure.html)
and [Remotion captions](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/captions/caption) provide useful
source-range/timeline/time-unit concepts, without imposing their formats on Vids.

[OpenMontage](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage) informed independently
written guidance about creative direction, concrete scene action and early
repetition review. Its code is not copied into these helpers. Its AGPLv3 license
is not asserted to govern our own implementations; a source link does not
authorize installing/running an upstream pipeline.

Metadata and a handful of frames cannot establish clean visuals, motion or sound.
Record the actual renderer/command and inspect the material. Links neither install
dependencies nor grant permissions.
